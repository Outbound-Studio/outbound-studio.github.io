Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · Applies to all mobile applications published by Outbound Studio (Vinc LLC)

1. Overview

Outbound Studio, operated by Vinc LLC ("we", "our", or "us"), respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you use our mobile applications on Android and iOS.

We do not require account registration and we do not operate backend servers that collect or store your personal information directly.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

App preferences, saved presets, audio or equipment profiles, and settings are stored locally on your device using standard on-device storage. This information stays on your device and is not transmitted to Outbound Studio servers.

Depending on the app, you can remove locally stored data at any time using the in-app reset options, clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or uninstalling the app.

3. Device Permissions

Some Outbound Studio apps request specific operating system permissions solely to deliver core features on your device — for example, detecting connected Bluetooth or wired audio devices to automatically apply your saved equalizer profile, or adjusting local audio effects. Information accessed through these permissions is processed on-device and is not collected or sold by us.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free versions of our apps may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. To deliver, personalize (where permitted), and measure ads, Google AdMob and its partners may collect and process certain device and usage data, including:

  • Advertising ID and device identifiers
  • IP address and coarse location derived from IP
  • Diagnostic data and ad interaction metrics

Where required by law (including in the EEA, UK, and applicable US states), our apps present a consent notice via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). You can review or update your ad privacy choices anytime in the app's Settings screen (where available) or opt out of personalized ads in your device's system settings.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner apps.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Our apps may offer optional one-time purchases (such as removing advertisements) or subscriptions. Payment transactions are processed securely by Google Play or the Apple App Store — we never receive or store your payment card details.

We use RevenueCat to validate receipts and manage purchase entitlements across app sessions and reinstalls. RevenueCat may process anonymous app user identifiers, purchase history, and device metadata necessary to verify and restore your purchases. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy for details.

6. Third-Party Services

Depending on the app, our mobile applications may integrate the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob & Google Play Services
  • RevenueCat
  • Apple App Store (for iOS apps)

7. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through one of our apps, please contact us so we can address it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our apps, legal requirements, or third-party integrations. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
